# 1,433 novel, catalytically intact cellulases from the global metagenome: a CAZyme-aware discovery screen with locked gates and decoy-controlled specificity

## Abstract

Cellulosic biofuel economics are enzyme-cost-limited, and the discovery of diverse cellulases - especially the exo-acting cellobiohydrolases that limit crystalline-cellulose saccharification - remains a bottleneck. We screened the MGnify30-C2 catalog (128.7M non-singleton proteins) with a 15-member, live-verified, CAZyme-family-spanning reference panel under a success gate locked before any candidate-level result was inspected: length, cellulase-GH Pfam-domain, homology (E<=1e-10), novelty (<40% identity to every reference), and catalytic Asp/Glu conservation at UniProt-annotated active-site positions, with three non-cellulase decoy panels (xylanase, lysozyme, amylase) run through the identical funnel as a specificity control. The screen yields 1,433 distinct novel cellulase candidates (96.9% below 30% identity to any reference), all catalytically intact; decoy yield is zero. Cross-catalog replication in the independent Pfam-poor MGnify30-C5-ppfam catalog recovers 30 candidates under the identical funnel, and composition-preserving scrambled controls produce zero hits against the full catalog. Structure templates at E<=1e-10 with >=60% coverage exist for 16 of the top-50. The pool is compositionally asymmetric in a commercially meaningful way: 84% are endoglucanases (GH5) and beta-glucosidases (GH3), while exo-acting cellobiohydrolases are scarce (GH7 3.0%, GH6 7.6%, GH48 1.3%) - identifying the 171-candidate exo-acting tranche as the high-value feedstock for cocktail engineering and the 672 beta-glucosidases as a screening library for glucose-tolerant variants.

## 1. Introduction

Enzymatic saccharification converts pretreated lignocellulose to fermentable sugars using cocktails of endoglucanases (EG; GH5, GH7, GH12, GH45), exo-cellobiohydrolases (CBH; GH7 CBH1, GH6 CBH2), beta-glucosidases (BGL; GH1, GH3), and processive accessory enzymes (GH9, GH48) (CAZy; literature ledger PMIDs). Industrial cocktails derive overwhelmingly from a few fungal hosts (Trichoderma reesei above all), and their cost per gallon of cellulosic ethanol remains a primary economic lever. Metagenomes sample microbial dark matter at scale (MGnify; Mitchell et al., PMID:31696235), and metagenomic cellulase discovery has repeatedly delivered industrial enzymes. The discovery problem is not sensitivity - cellulases are abundant in environmental sequence - but specificity and validation: glycoside hydrolases share folds and catalytic machinery across substrate classes (xylanases, amylases, lysozymes), so sequence-similarity screens risk family-bleed false positives, and candidate lists without decoy controls, catalytic-residue verification, and independent replication are not decision-grade. We therefore locked a CAZyme-family-aware protocol - reference panel spanning 10 GH families with UniProt ACT_SITE grounding, catalytic-acid/base conservation as an explicit filter, three non-cellulase decoy panels through the identical funnel, independent-catalog replication, and scrambled-sequence controls - before inspecting any candidate.

## 2. Methods

Fifteen characterized cellulases (GH3/5/6/7/9/12/45/48; UniProt accessions, active-site annotations, and retrieval hashes in the reference manifest) were searched against MGnify30-C2 by phmmer (EBI HMMER API; Potter et al., PMID:29905871). Locked filters: F1 length 180-900 aa; F2 hit-metadata Pfam includes a locked catalytic-cellulase family set; F3 min E<=1e-10; F4 max global identity to any reference <40% (BLOSUM62 -11/-1); F6 Asp/Glu conservation at every annotated catalytic position of the best-E ACT_SITE reference (mapping vehicle substitution documented: EBI afa downloads omit the query row, so positions were mapped through a pairwise global alignment with the identical aligner). Decoys (Trichoderma xylanase, hen lysozyme, Bacillus amylase) were screened identically. Validation: independent replication of the full screen in MGnify30-C5-ppfam; five composition-preserving scrambled candidates searched against the full catalog; top-20 candidates screened against UniProtKB. Thermostability proxy: aliphatic index >= reference-panel median AND >=2 cysteines. Structure: RCSB sequence search of the top-50, E<=1e-10, >=60% local coverage.

## 3. Results

**Specificity-controlled discovery.** 12,451 unique hits funnel to 1,433 final candidates (F1 9,057; F2 5,033; F3 3,401; F4 3,395; F6 1,433). All six locked gates pass: >=12 references (15); >=40 candidates (1,433); >=15 at <30% novelty (1,389); >=15/50 structure templates (16); >=5 thermostability-proxy (1,266); decoy yield <=10% of reference yield (0 vs 95.5/query).

**Novelty.** 96.9% of candidates are below 30% identity to every reference; UniProtKB screening of the top-20 confirms novelty beyond the reference panel: of 15 completed screens, median best-homolog identity is 36.1%; three candidates match existing metagenome-derived UniProt deposits (99.7%, 98.5%, 100%) and are flagged as already-known; one has no UniProtKB hit at E<=1e-40. Five screens were blocked by an EBI API incident and are documented as such - no gate depends on this screen.

**Controls.** All 5 scrambled controls: zero hits at E<=1e-10 against 128.7M sequences. Cross-catalog replication: 30 candidates under the identical funnel in the Pfam-poor C5 catalog (a lower bound, since F2's Pfam requirement is harder to satisfy there by construction).

**Composition.** GH3 672; GH5 530; GH6 109; GH45 56; GH7 43; GH48 19; multi-domain 4. Exo-acting tranche (GH7+GH6+GH48) = 171 candidates (11.9%).

## 4. Discussion

The locked decoy design is what makes the count meaningful: xylanase, lysozyme, and amylase queries - all glycoside hydrolases with shared catalytic chemistry - produce zero gate-counted candidates through the identical funnel, so the 1,433 cannot be attributed to family bleed. The catalytic-conservation filter (F6) removes 58% of otherwise-passing homologs (3,395 -> 1,433), most plausibly pseudogene fragments and catalytically degenerate homologs; what remains is a conservative, experiment-ready pool.

The compositional asymmetry is the commercially actionable result. Metagenomes are rich in endoglucanases and beta-glucosidases and poor in exo-cellobiohydrolases, mirroring why CBH1 dominates cocktail cost. The 171-candidate exo-acting tranche - 43 GH7s among them, all catalytically intact and <40% identical to Tre CBH1-class enzymes - is the direct discovery feedstock for next-generation cocktails, and the 672 GH3s a library for glucose-tolerance screens. Sixteen top-50 candidates are already structure-modelable at high coverage, enabling structure-guided triage before expression. Immediate next steps: expression of the exo-acting tranche plus thermostable GH3 picks, filter-paper and cellobiose-release assays on pretreated biomass, and cocktail reconstitution against a commercial baseline.
